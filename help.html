Help & printing

Print your folded program

  1. Download the PDF after publishing your program.
  2. Use the paper size chosen in Program Studio: Legal (8.5 × 14 inches) for the tri-fold or Letter (8.5 × 11 inches) for the standard bi-fold.
  3. Choose landscape orientation and Actual size or 100%. Turn off Fit to page.
  4. Print both pages double-sided and choose Flip on short edge.
  5. Print one test copy and check orientation before printing the full batch.
  6. Fold the Legal program into three panels, or fold the Standard program in half.

If your printer does not support automatic double-sided printing, follow its manual-duplex instructions and test how it feeds paper.
